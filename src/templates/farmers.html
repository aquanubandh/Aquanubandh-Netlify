FAQ

Questions from farmers

Is Aquanubandh Plus the best free shrimp app for Indian farmers?
Yes. Aquanubandh Plus is a free shrimp farming software solution in India, designed for Vannamei pond management, attendance, inventory, and market access without expensive hardware or sensors.
Does the app work offline and track attendance and inventory?
Yes. You can log daily pond feed, mark staff attendance, and manage your feed and input inventory completely offline in the field. Your data syncs automatically when you reconnect.
Can I buy PL, sell my harvest, and get live prices in the app?
Absolutely. Aquanubandh Plus lets you check live Vannamei market prices by count and region, order quality Post-Larvae (PL), and connect directly to sell your harvest.
How does Aquanubandh Plus differ from other shrimp apps like Jala, Tomota, or Aquaconnect?
Unlike apps that require you to buy expensive hardware, Aquanubandh Plus is a software-only solution that combines offline pond logging, attendance, and inventory with live prices, PL buying, and harvest selling on your smartphone.
