It began with a simple need: my father wanted an Excel sheet to track pond feed logs and expenses. The Feed Wastage Problem

The Hidden Cost of Excess Feed

Why improper feed management is the silent crop killer in Indian aquaculture.

In Vannamei shrimp farming, feed accounts for over 60% of total operational costs. However, a significant portion of this feed is often wasted due to overfeeding, inaccurate biomass estimation, or lack of real-time monitoring. When excess feed goes uneaten, it sinks to the pond bottom and decays, forming toxic sludge.

This decaying organic matter triggers a chain reaction: it depletes dissolved oxygen (DO) levels, raises lethal ammonia and hydrogen sulfide levels, and forces farmers to spend heavily on water exchange and expensive probiotics. A high Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR) not only eats directly into crop margins but also severely compromises shrimp health, making them highly vulnerable to diseases like WSSV and EMS.

Reducing FCR by even 0.1 can save lakhs of rupees per crop while keeping the pond environment pristine.
