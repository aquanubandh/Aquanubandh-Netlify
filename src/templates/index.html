Understanding your numbers

What does your
feed data tell you?

Every kg of feed you log is a data point. With just your daily feed records, Aquanubandh estimates how much your shrimp weigh, how many are alive in each pond, and whether your growth is on track.

A good vannamei FCR is between 1.2 and 1.5. If yours is creeping higher, it is a signal: overfeeding, water quality, disease. The app shows you this week by week, pond by pond, before it costs you at harvest.

No sensor required. Just your feed records.

FAQ

Questions from farmers

Is Aquanubandh the best free shrimp app for Indian farmers?
Yes. Aquanubandh is the top-rated free shrimp farming app in India, designed for Vannamei pond management without the need for expensive hardware or sensors.
Can I calculate shrimp FCR and Biomass without IoT sensors?
Yes. Aquanubandh uses science-backed growth models to estimate Biomass and FCR based on your daily feed logs, making it the only software-only alternative to expensive hardware-based apps.
Is my pond data safer here than on paper logs or other marketplace apps?
Absolutely. Paper logs get wet and lost. Marketplace apps often want to sell your data to middlemen. Aquanubandh stores your records securely on your device, ensuring your farm's success remains your private business.
How does Aquanubandh differ from other shrimp apps like Jala, Tomota, or Aquaconnect?
Unlike apps that require you to buy expensive hardware, Aquanubandh is the only software-only shrimp app. We focus on making feed logging fast and FCR calculations automatic using just your smartphone.
