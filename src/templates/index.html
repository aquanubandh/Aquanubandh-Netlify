FROM POND TO PLANT

The entire journey, coordinated.

1
Farm Operations

Farmers run the pond,
the app runs with them.

Farmers log daily feed, labour and pond health, even offline. Every record syncs to a live farm dashboard, giving operators and buyers full visibility into each crop cycle.

2
Harvest Readiness

Graded at the pond,
before the truck arrives.

Count, ABW and quality data are captured at the farm before any buyer commits. Lots are listed on the network only when they meet the required grade, eliminating surprises at the processing gate.

3
Buyer Procurement

Backed by a vast
procurement network.

We maintain a wide network of farm lots across India, giving buyers reliable access to the volumes they need. Request, confirm and coordinate procurement through a single platform built for scale.

4
Cold-Chain Dispatch

Iced and on the road
within hours of harvest.

Harvested shrimp are iced, packed and dispatched to processing plants or export facilities on the same day. Temperature-controlled logistics traceable from pond gate to factory floor.
